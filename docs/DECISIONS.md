# Decisions

Status values: DECIDED (owner-approved), PROPOSED, OPEN.

## D1. Target audience and launch community - DECIDED
Open to everyone interested in healthspan: connecting and discussing longevity, youth, healthcare,
biotech, DNA and genomics, proteins, brain capability, appearance, human performance, healing and
related topics. There is no single launch community; the product launches to the general public.
- Social Portal and Personal Portal are open to every member.
- Researcher Portal spaces are gated by verification and permissions, not by audience.

## D2. Geographic scope - DECIDED (compliance review required)
Available to everyone with no regional restriction or geoblocking. Users can apply their own
filters (language, region, topic, evidence class, and similar) to shape what they see; filters are
personal preferences, never access restrictions.
- Onboarding: Require users to check an acknowledgment of legal obligations before completing the
  modal. Checking it completes that acknowledgment step only; it does not replace the platform's
  legal review or compliance obligations.
- Localization: language is English for now.

## D3. MVP boundary - DECIDED
The MVP is basic but working across the main areas; depth comes later.
- Use the M4 scope in `DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md` as the boundary: working Social and Personal
  experiences, controlled Researcher collaboration and basic organization profiles, events,
  General Platform AI, and a restricted Private AI Scientist prototype, with governance and
  moderation foundations.
- Include basic direct and group text messaging. Defer rich media messaging, full health-record
  management, payments, and Organization-Specialized AI as specified by the roadmap.
- Launch Social and Personal access to the general public. Researcher spaces remain permission-gated.

## M1 decision status

D1-D3 resolve the audience, geographic availability, and MVP-boundary decisions required by M1.
Applicable legal and regulatory requirements remain subject to review before launch; the
geographic decision does not waive them. Other decisions listed in the roadmap remain open until
their stated milestones.
