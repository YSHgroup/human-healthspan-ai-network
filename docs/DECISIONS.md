# Decisions

Status values: DECIDED (owner-approved), PROPOSED, OPEN.

## D1. Target audience and launch community - DECIDED
Open to everyone interested in healthspan: connecting and discussing longevity, youth, healthcare,
biotech, DNA and genomics, proteins, brain capability, appearance, human performance, healing and
related topics. There is no single launch community; the product launches to the general public.
- Social Portal and Personal Portal are open to every member.
- Researcher Portal spaces are gated by verification and permissions, not by audience.

## D2. Geographic scope - DECIDED (compliance review required)
Available to everyone with no regional restriction or geoblocking. Users can apply their own
filters (language, region, topic, evidence class, and similar) to shape what they see; filters are
personal preferences, never access restrictions.
- Onboarding: Require users to check an acknowledgment of legal obligations before completing the
  modal. Checking it completes that acknowledgment step only; it does not replace the platform's
  legal review or compliance obligations.
- Localization: language is English for now.

## D3. MVP boundary - DECIDED
The MVP is basic but working across the main areas; depth comes later.
- Use the M4 scope in `DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md` as the boundary: working Social and Personal
  experiences, controlled Researcher collaboration and basic organization profiles, events,
  General Platform AI, and a restricted Private AI Scientist prototype, with governance and
  moderation foundations.
- Include basic direct and group text messaging. Defer rich media messaging, full health-record
  management, payments, and Organization-Specialized AI as specified by the roadmap.
- Launch Social and Personal access to the general public. Researcher spaces remain permission-gated.

## M1 decision status

D1-D3 resolve the audience, geographic availability, and MVP-boundary decisions required by M1.
Applicable legal and regulatory requirements remain subject to review before launch; the
geographic decision does not waive them. Additional approved M2 policy decisions are D4-D6 below.
Other roadmap decisions remain open until their stated milestones.

## D4. Research community membership - DECIDED
Open communities can be joined by any registered member. Restricted research communities require
an application and moderator approval. Approval grants membership in that community only; it does
not grant access to every project or resource associated with it.

## D5. Community moderation - DECIDED
Publish community rules and allow members to report posts and comments. Authorized moderators may
hide or remove content, issue warnings, and temporarily suspend posting. Serious cases escalate to
platform administrators, and members have an appeal path.

## D6. Activity-based profile badges - DECIDED
Award visible Contributor and Research Contributor badges for qualifying public, published work
and research achievements. Publish the award criteria. Badges recognize contributions and do not
assert professional credentials or qualifications. Remove eligibility when the qualifying work is
removed or invalidated.

## D7. Creation entitlements and organization onboarding - DECIDED
Any member may submit a free request to create a community or organization. Starting in M5, actual
creation requires either an active premium entitlement or a one-time purchase entitlement. Before
M5, do not launch checkout or charge users; use admin-granted entitlements for development and
pilots. Organization activation additionally requires platform-admin verification of basic
identity and website. Organization membership is invite-based with owner, admin, and member roles,
and organization data is strictly tenant-isolated. Payment never bypasses organization verification
or access controls.

## D8. Admin AI cost reporting - DECIDED
Defer provider cost reporting to M4. M3 Admin acceptance covers aggregate interaction usage,
outcomes, character volume, and latency without estimating costs. Add cost reporting only when
reliable provider usage and pricing inputs are available.
