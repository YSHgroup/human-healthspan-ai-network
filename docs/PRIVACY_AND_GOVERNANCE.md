# Privacy and Governance

**Status: principles plus limited AI-learning consent capture.** Health-data consent, retention
and review workflows remain planned. Geographic availability is approved without geoblocking;
applicable legal and regulatory review remains required before public launch.

## Principles

- Personal health information is private by default and is not research material or AI training
  data unless separate, explicit consent and governance say so.
- Popularity is not scientific evidence. Evidence classes and provenance are shown wherever
  science is discussed. AI hypotheses are proposals, not discoveries.
- Organization and project boundaries are enforced for data and for AI retrieval.
- Sensitive trend or interest inference is limited to explicit settings and aggregated signals,
  with member control and transparency.

## Health data

Private by default; explicit sharing (private, connections, specific people or researchers,
authorized organizations, time-limited links); scoped, expiring, revocable links; access
auditing; consent records; retention rules; secure file storage. Features beyond foundations
wait until consent, sharing and audit are verified (M5).

## Research and AI governance

Ownership and attribution, source references, version history and auditability. AI
governance enforces permission-aware retrieval, provenance, organization isolation, purpose
limitation, training-data permissions, evaluation and monitoring. Scientific oversight
(molecular biology, genetics, medicine, aging, AI, statistics, epidemiology, bioethics)
is established as the research ecosystem matures (M6).

The development AI endpoint records metadata for each attempt. Prompt and response content is not
stored unless the member affirmatively opts in to the versioned AI-learning consent. Members may
revoke consent; revocation clears previously retained content. Retained content is not
training-eligible, and no training/export workflow consumes it. The external AI provider still
receives the current prompt history when configured; learning consent is separate from provider
processing and does not opt users out of that request processing.

## Premium and monetization

Premium access is decided only by the backend entitlement system. Monetization must not
undermine scientific credibility, privacy or trust; sensitive data is never an unrestricted
commercial asset.
